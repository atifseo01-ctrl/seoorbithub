Technical SEO overview

Technical SEO improves the systems and structure behind your content. We look for barriers that can affect discovery, usability and the way search engines interpret your site.

What we review

  • Website crawling, indexation and site architecture.
  • Core Web Vitals, page speed and mobile SEO.
  • Structured data and relevant schema implementation.
  • XML sitemap, robots.txt and canonicalization.
  • Broken links, redirects, duplicate content and URL consistency.
  • Technical SEO audits, monitoring and prioritized recommendations.

How we improve technical foundations

We begin with a crawl and technical review, validate findings against search behavior, prioritize by impact and effort, then support implementation and monitoring.

Frequently asked questions

Is technical SEO only for large websites?

No. Every site benefits from a clear, crawlable and usable foundation. The depth of work depends on the site, platform and goals.

Will an audit fix issues automatically?

An audit identifies and explains opportunities. We can help prioritize and guide implementation, but changes require review and deployment.
